PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY · 27 AUGUST 2026

Privacy

Your study material belongs on your device. We designed the product around that fact.

What stays local

Your browser stores prompt sets, typed responses, ratings, recaps, theme preference, and the license token. We do not receive this study data. You can export it, import it, or clear it from the Library.

What leaves the device

The server may record an IP address, timestamp, requested path, and user agent for security and delivery. We do not add usage reports, advertising trackers, or third-party fonts and scripts.

If you verify a Contour license, your browser sends only the license token to the Sociobot billing API. Study prompts and responses are never sent with that request. Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record, process checkout and license status under their own required payment records.

Offline cache

The offline feature stores basic app pages and artwork on your device. Clear this site’s browser data to remove the cache and all local records.

Your choices

You can use the full study flow without an account or purchase. Export is never paywalled. Browser private mode or storage-clearing settings may remove local data, so download a backup if it matters to you.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
